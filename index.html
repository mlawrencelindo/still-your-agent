The gap

Clients say they will come back. Few of them do.

91%

of buyers say they would use their agent again or recommend them.

15 to 18%

of buyers actually went back to an agent they had worked with before.

Source: NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, as summarized by Virginia Realtors. Reported figures for repeat use vary between 15% and 18%. The usual reason is simple: the client lost touch, and another agent was there first.

Check-in email

Home anniversary notes

I do

You do

Local market video

I do

You do
Call those people. That is where the deals come from.
Setup, first week

One week to get your list ready. Then the monthly rhythm starts.

  1. You upload your list

    Export your past clients and sphere from your CRM or phone as a CSV, and drop it in the shared Google Drive folder.

  2. I clean it

    Duplicates removed, missing closing dates filled, every contact tagged as past client or sphere.

  3. I learn your voice

    You share 3 to 5 emails or texts you have sent clients. Everything I write sounds like those.

  4. Your calendar is set

    Every home anniversary for the year, mapped from closing dates, so no client gets missed.

Who it fits

Built for agents who work alone and have a list they never get to.

A good fit

  • You work solo, with no team or assistant.
  • You have been in the business 5 years or more.
  • You have 200 to 1,000 past clients and sphere contacts.
  • You close about 8 to 20 deals a year.

Not a fit

  • You want cold calling or bought leads.
  • You need someone on live calls during your workday.
  • You already have a team member doing past-client follow-up.
Questions

Before you start

Do you contact my clients?

No. You send everything, from your own email, CRM, and phone. Your clients only ever hear from you.

Do you need my CRM login?

No. You export your list and upload it to a shared Google Drive folder. I never ask for passwords.

Will it sound like me?

I write from 3 to 5 messages you have already sent clients. You approve every piece before it goes out, and you can edit anything.

How much of my time does it take?

About 30 minutes a month to read, approve, and send, plus the calls you choose to make from the report.

Do we need to get on calls?

No. The work runs through the shared folder and email. If you want a quick video walkthrough of the first month, I can record one.

What if I want to stop?

Tell me before your next month starts. There is no contract, and your cleaned list and anniversary calendar stay yours.
