Every month

Four things land in your shared folder each month.

  • Check-in email

    I do
    Write one email in your voice. Useful, short, never a sales pitch.
    You do
    Read it, approve it, send it to your list from your own email or CRM.

  • Home anniversary notes

    I do
    Write a short personal note for each client whose closing anniversary falls that month.
    You do
    Tap each note on your phone. A text opens with the note written and your client's number filled in. You hit send.

  • Local market video

    I do
    Edit one 60-second video on your local market from public numbers.
    You do
    Post it or text it to your list. Your face is optional. See the sample below.

  • One-page report

    I do
    Read every reply you forward and list who mentioned buying, selling, or a friend who is.
    You do
    Forward replies to me (a one-time email rule does it for you). Then call the people on the list. That is where the deals come from.

About 30 minutes a month of your time, plus the calls you choose to make. I email you when your month is ready, and again a few days later as a reminder.

Setup, first week

One week to get your list ready. Then the monthly rhythm starts.

  1. You share a folder

    Make a folder in your own Google Drive and share it with me. Export your past clients and sphere from your CRM or phone as a CSV, and drop it in.

  2. I clean it

    Duplicates removed, missing closing dates filled, every contact tagged as past client or sphere.

  3. I learn your voice

    You share 3 to 5 emails or texts you have sent clients, plus the brokerage and license details your ads need. Everything I write sounds like you and carries them.

  4. Your calendar is set

    Every home anniversary for the year, mapped from closing dates, so no client gets missed.

Your client list stays yours

It lives in a folder in your own Google Drive, and you control who can open it. I never sell it, share it, or email anyone on it. When you stop, you unshare the folder and I delete anything I downloaded that same day.

Questions

Before you start

Is this for me?

A good fit

  • You work solo, with no team or assistant.
  • You have been in the business 5 years or more.
  • You have 200 to 1,000 past clients and sphere contacts.
  • You close about 8 to 20 deals a year.

Not a fit

  • You want cold calling or bought leads.
  • You need someone on live calls during your workday.
  • You already have a team member doing past-client follow-up.
Do you contact my clients?
No. You send everything, from your own email, CRM, and phone. Your clients only ever hear from you.
Do you need my CRM login?
No. You export your list and put it in a folder in your Google Drive that you share with me. I never ask for passwords.
If you never see my inbox, how do you know who replied?
Replies come to you, since you send everything. You set up one email rule that forwards replies to the monthly check-in to me (I send you the two-minute steps for Gmail or Outlook), or you forward them by hand. Text replies to anniversary notes stay on your phone. Tell me about any that matter and I add them to the report.
What happens to my client list?
It stays in a folder in your own Google Drive, and you decide who can open it. I never sell it, share it, or email anyone on it. When you stop, you unshare the folder and I delete anything I downloaded that same day.
I already have Homebot or my CRM's drip emails. Why add this?
Those tools send the same automated email to everyone. This is written in your voice, includes a personal note for each home anniversary, and comes with a person reading the replies and telling you who to call. It works fine alongside them.
Can't I just use ChatGPT for this?
You can use it to write an email. The writing was never the hard part. The hard part is a clean list, knowing whose anniversary is this month, doing it every single month, and reading the replies to spot who wants to move. That is what you pay for. I use AI tools to work faster, and I check every word and every number myself before anything reaches you.
Will this follow my brokerage and state rules?
Every email and video carries your name, brokerage, and license details the way your brokerage requires. You give me the wording at setup. Market numbers come from public sources like the Redfin Data Center, never from your MLS, and each one comes with its source. You approve everything before it goes out, so the final say stays with you.
Will it sound like me?
I write from 3 to 5 messages you have already sent clients. You approve every piece before it goes out, and you can edit anything.
How much of my time does it take?
About 30 minutes a month to read, approve, and send, plus the calls you choose to make from the report.
Do we need to get on calls?
No. The work runs through the shared folder and email. If you want a quick video walkthrough of the first month, I can record one.
What if I want to stop?
Tell me before your next month starts. There is no contract, and your cleaned list and anniversary calendar stay yours.
